Mishkal Tahor Beta - Windows
============================

Installing
1. Double-click "Install Mishkal Tahor Beta Vxx.exe".
   Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't signed yet:
   click "More info", then "Run anyway".
2. Click through the installer. It puts the plug-in in C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins (Ableton: Options > Preferences > Plug-ins > Rescan;
   FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find plugins).
4. Load "Mishkal Tahor" on a track.
5. Open the User Manual PDF in this folder for a tour of every section and the shortcuts.

Getting started
- Flip between RESO, TAHOR and CHARACTER with the arrows at the top.
- TAHOR: double-click the screen to add a band; touch a dot to open its panel; right-click a dot for its menu.
- CHARACTER starts empty: "+ Add EQ" opens the gear (16 vintage-style units).
- Click the preset name for 69 presets in folders (Vocals, Drums, Instruments, Mastering, Mix Bus, Surgical,
  Reso Clean-up, Character, Effects), or the star for the browser.
- MATCH and AUTO GAIN keep the level fair while you compare.

Things we'd love you to try
- A dynamic Tahor band on a harsh vocal - grab its dashed range outline anywhere and drag to set the range.
- The band bubble: click the shape to change it (Bell, shelf, cut...), click the frequency, gain or Q to type
  a value ("2.5k", "A4", "-3", "0.7"), and hit S to solo. It stays open while you hover it.
- Hover the empty screen: a readout shows the frequency and note under the mouse.
- Spectrum Grab: hover a peak in the analyzer and drag it - it becomes a band.
- EQ MATCH with a reference track in the sidechain.

Requirements: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, a VST3 host.

Uninstalling
Settings > Apps > "Mishkal Tahor Beta" > Uninstall.
