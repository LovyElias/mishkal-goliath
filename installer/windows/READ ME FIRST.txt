Mishkal Goliath Beta - Windows
==============================

Installing
1. Double-click "Install Mishkal Goliath Beta Vxx.exe".
   Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't signed yet:
   click "More info", then "Run anyway".
2. Click through the installer. It puts the plug-in in C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins (Ableton: Options > Preferences > Plug-ins > Rescan;
   FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find plugins).
4. Load "Mishkal Goliath Beta" on a track.
5. Open the User Manual PDF in this folder for a tour of every section and tips.

Requirements: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, a VST3 host.

Uninstalling
Settings > Apps > "Mishkal Goliath Beta" > Uninstall.
